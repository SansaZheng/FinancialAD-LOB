# Components and matched-cohort comparisons

This stage addresses three concrete weaknesses in the previous evidence: the enhanced feature bundle was not separated into components; cascade and direct scores were evaluated on different source cohorts; CPU reloads of enhanced HGB were not reliable. Stage012 results and its failed reload attempt remain immutable development evidence.

The registered dataset uses five WSE stocks on twelve previously unused dates. Training: February1–3,2017. Validation: February6–8. Test: February9,10,13,14,15,16. Full daily partitions are acquired through CQ and independently replayed before labeling. The same passive lifecycle generator uses seed17090. These are semi-synthetic policy labels, not adjudicated trading intent. Six distinct test dates improve coverage but do not imply statistically independent market conditions.

All methods use identical chronological/held-out-stock folds, training IDs, uniform6000-window cap and five seeds. Combined folds exclude both the test stock and the next alphabetical validation stock from training. No validation/test row fits a normalizer, representation, classifier or hard-negative miner. No hard weights are used in this stage, given the mixed stage012 evidence and the need to isolate feature families.

Nine HGB variants comprise the complete2^3factorial of Gram100, lagged co-occurrence300 and statistics88 added to the same base1625 inputs, plus a pure per-level80-coordinate control. Statistics88 contains80per-level quantities and8pooled synchrony/breadth values. The additional control was declared before any new-data fit, with the original protocol preserved. Gram and lagged matrices include both diagonal and off-diagonal terms: these are feature-family ablations, not an isolated proof that off-diagonal terms alone are necessary.

Five neural/pipeline comparisons complete the14-method matrix: base-input directMLP, all-feature directMLP, price-aligned cascade+OC-SVM, its no-contrastive ablation, and a directBCEhead with the same frozenLOBrepresentation and same fusionencoder dimensions/initialization. The cascade is the stronger price-aligned WSE adaptation from this project, not an exact reproduction of the private original-paper setup. The shared head also uses the same balanced class sampling schedule; different loss functions and final detectors remain part of the pipeline contrast.

Every method has six validation-only trials and the same strict validation-calibrated1%negative-FPR threshold rule. Direct models use20epochs per trial. The shared LOBautoencoder uses10epochs and each cascade fusion20epochs, followed by six OC-SVM trials. The cascade representation follows the fixed previous hyperparameters; its six trials tune the detector, whereas direct trials tune the supervised model. Equal trial counts do not mean equal FLOPs or equally extensive representation searches. All trial losses, times, parameter counts, choices, predictions and selected models are archived, and these limits must accompany any pipeline conclusion. Main component comparisons have matched HGB budgets throughout.

Primary contrast: full enhanced HGB versus the88-coordinate statistics control. Additional contrasts include full versus ordinaryHGB, perlevel80 versus statistics88, each factorial marginal effect averaged over the other two factors, the Gram×lag interaction, and matched-cohort cascade/direct comparisons. Results report macro stock-day AP, pooled-fold AP, seed SD, benign/backgroundFPR and recall. Seeds are averaged before exploratory resampling of dates and stocks; secondary intervals are not multiplicity-adjusted. Fourteen variants are not fourteen independent published algorithms.

Portability is a prerequisite, not a score-tuning operation. See docs/portable-013.md. Interaction features use float64 with fixed quantization; HGB uses numeric tree exports and no scaler; neural and kernel scores use numeric exports with canonical NumPy inference. First qualify frozen synthetic models in CQGPU, CQCPU and Mac environments, then audit actual model reloads. Preserve failed checks, exact code/config/data hashes and threshold-flip counts. Do not retune or relax acceptance because of held-out performance.

There are30registered fold-seed runs and420selected evaluations. CQ uses free resources only; initial remaining budget6.52GPUhours. A full-data pilot determines runtime allocations, never algorithm settings or whether to suppress a weak method. Primary fitting code is frozen before that pilot; all remaining folds/seeds follow regardless of its performance, subject to the real resource limits. No paid cloud, unrelated job changes or environment modifications are authorized.
